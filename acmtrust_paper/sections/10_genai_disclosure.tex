\section*{GenAI Usage Disclosure}
%% Required by ACM TRUST 2027; exempt from the page limit.

Generative AI was used in this work in three ways.

\paragraph{As objects of study.} Claude Sonnet~5.5, Claude Haiku~5.5, gpt-oss-120b, Gemma~4 31B and Qwen3.5~9B built the simulator programs we evaluate. Claude Haiku~5.5 interpreted every question, produced the reading answers, and performed \otf's record edits. These five models also served as the LLM-based judges described in \S\ref{sec:judges}, and TypeSafe's Jev, a hosted decision model that writes no text, served as one further judge. All model calls, tokens, costs and failures are logged in the artifact; for Jev, seven of 3{,}410 call-ledger lines were lost to parallel writes, and each judgment row still records its calls and cost.
